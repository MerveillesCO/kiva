# Kiva Demand and Pricing Validation Plan

## Decision to validate

Kiva needs evidence for two different products:

1. a 6–10 guest seasonal pilot that validates the food and hosted experience; and
2. a mature 30–40 guest experience capable of supporting staff, fixed costs, and
   a meaningful share of permanent capital.

Interest in an inexpensive pilot cannot validate the permanent business. Record
each product, setting, duration, inclusions, cancellation terms, and price exactly.

This table assumes every stated cover is sold and served. It excludes financing,

“Operating contribution” means revenue less cover-variable costs, nightly direct

plan excludes annual fixed operating cost and must not be used for the permanent
35 percent margin gate.
The 35 percent operating-margin threshold is approximately $132 per cover. A 35
percent margin after the illustrative capital-recovery charge is approximately
$159 per cover.

## Market reference points

These are positioning references, not directly comparable products:

- Ephemera in Colorado Springs publishes a five-course tasting menu at $79,
  excluding its optional pairing.
- SHEEP's 2026 Denver open-fire four-course residency launched at $95.
- Broadway 10 Denver publishes chef-table dinner options at $90, $125, and $150.
- Dine with Roilty publishes private-chef starting prices of $125 for three
  courses and $175 for five courses.
- Urban Farmer Denver published a one-night four-course paired event at $220,
  inclusive of taxes, fees, and gratuity.

Kiva's rural setting, shared table, smoke craft, small capacity, and possible
AfterFaire relationship can justify an experience premium only if service,
comfort, sanitation, arrival, storytelling, and food quality feel intentional.
The unfinished-site novelty alone does not justify the permanent-case price.

## Four-step validation

### 1. Concept interviews

Interview at least 30 qualified prospects: Renaissance Festival visitors,
Colorado Springs/Monument destination diners, private-dinner buyers, and nearby
hosts. Show one-page concepts without asking “Would you pay?” first. Ask them to
describe the occasion, alternatives, travel radius, party size, concerns, and
expected price. Then reveal price ranges in randomized order.

Keep friends and family results separate from unaffiliated prospects.

### 2. Deposit-backed landing tests

Test three truthful offers with identical imagery and cancellation terms:

- Garden Smokehouse Supper: $95
- Kiva Hearth Table: $125
- Kiva Chef's Table: $150

Use refundable deposits rather than email interest. Do not offer dates before the
land-use and food pathways authorize sale. If approvals are pending, use a
clearly labeled no-charge waitlist and do not imply the event is scheduled.

Minimum useful sample: 100 qualified visits per offer or enough traffic to produce
at least 20 deposit decisions. Record source, party size, conversion, abandonment,
refund, and stated objection. This is a directional test, not a statistically
conclusive market study.

### 3. Permitted service tests

For each approved service, record offered seats, deposits, served covers, price,
discount, refund, food cost, consumables, labor minutes, fuel, waste, guest score,
and repeat/referral intent. Never combine free rehearsal guests with paid demand.

Run at least three services at the same price before interpreting performance.
Change only one major variable at a time: price, menu, duration, or inclusions.

### 4. Mature-format presales

Before permanent design, test the actual 30–40-cover product through a lawful
rented venue, caterer partnership, or other approved location. Sell at least four
services at the proposed permanent price and staffing level. Ten-person sellouts
do not prove 36-person operations or demand.

## Demand gates

### Pilot gate

- At least 70 percent of offered seats are deposit-backed at $95 or more.
- At least 80 percent of paid guests rate the experience 4/5 or better.
- Plate-level food and consumable cost stays within the approved budget.
- No price relies on illegal operation, undisclosed donation expectations, or
  unapproved lodging/event bundling.

### Seasonal gate

- Twelve compliant permitted services completed.
- Median paid attendance reaches at least 80 percent of capacity.
- At least 25 percent of unaffiliated guests join another event, refer a paid
  guest, or place a deposit within 90 days.
- Contribution is positive after all event labor, permit allocation, insurance,
  fuel, sanitation, refunds, and payment costs.

### Permanent gate

- Four lawful 30–40-cover services sell at $132 or more without broad discounting.
- A $150–$160 offer demonstrates credible conversion, or construction scope is
  reduced until a lower supported price meets the return requirement.
- The trailing model reaches at least 35 percent operating contribution using
  actual labor and fixed costs.
- The market is not dependent solely on eight Renaissance Faire weekends.

## Offer architecture

Keep food price independent of lodging and alcohol in the model:

- Core supper covers food, nonalcoholic beverage, service, and experience.
- Lodging, if approved, is a separate line with its own taxes, cancellation, and
  zoning basis.
- Alcohol is excluded until licensing and service responsibilities are resolved.
- Gratuity, taxes, fees, and required donations must be disclosed consistently.
