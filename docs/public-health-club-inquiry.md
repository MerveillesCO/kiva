# Draft Public Health Inquiry — Merveilles Club Suppers

**To:** El Paso County Public Health, Environmental Health Division  
**Subject:** Written classification request — nonprofit social-club member suppers
at 21085 Capella Drive

Hello,

I own and reside at 21085 Capella Drive, Monument, Colorado, parcel 7103005001.
Before forming an organization, advertising events, accepting money, preparing
food, or investing in equipment, I am requesting written guidance about a
possible Merveilles cultural and social club.

Merveilles may be organized as a Colorado nonprofit membership corporation whose
purpose would include historical foodways, hearth and smoke cooking, traditional
crafts, seasonal gatherings, workshops, and Renaissance Faire community. It
would have limited individual membership, bylaws, member governance, annual dues,
and a program broader than meals. It might hold up to eight member supper events
in the first summer, for no more than 10 members and bona fide member guests per
event. Meals could have a separate event charge. No alcohol is proposed.

Colorado's definition of “retail food establishment” appears to exclude certain
food donation, preparation, sale, or service by a nonprofit or charitable
organization in conjunction with an event or celebration, provided the activity
does not exceed the event or celebration or 52 days in a calendar year and occurs
in the organization's home county. We do not assume this exclusion applies.

Could you please clarify:

1. Would a Colorado nonprofit social club qualify as a “nonprofit or charitable
   organization” for this provision, or is an additional tax-exempt status or
   organizational purpose required?
2. Can a recurring, scheduled members' supper be an “event or celebration,” or is
   the exclusion limited to independently occurring events such as fundraisers or
   festivals?
3. Does charging members for the supper, paying a chef, or allowing bona fide
   guests affect eligibility?
4. If the exclusion applies, may any food be prepared in the owner's residential
   kitchen? If not, must preparation occur through a licensed caterer, commissary,
   or licensed temporary setup?
5. Do potentially hazardous foods, smoking meat for flavor, hot/cold holding, or
   food prepared in advance require licensing, plan review, HACCP review, or other
   approval despite the organizational structure?
6. What requirements would apply to the domestic well, handwashing sink,
   wastewater, utensil washing, portable toilets, refuse, outdoor smoker, covered
   service area, and employee restroom access?
7. If the nonprofit-event provision does not apply, which path should we evaluate:
   licensed caterer, temporary/special-event retail food establishment, mobile
   unit, or fixed retail food establishment?
8. What application or written determination should we request before proceeding?

We are separately requesting land-use classification from El Paso County Planning
and understand that a Public Health answer would not authorize the use of the
property. We will not operate until both agencies identify a lawful path.
