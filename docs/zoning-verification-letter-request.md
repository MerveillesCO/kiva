## Request

Please issue a Zoning Verification Letter identifying the applicable land-use
classification and approval path for each scenario below. We are seeking zoning
verification before investing in food permits, equipment, or construction. This
request is not intended to combine the scenarios into one immediate application.

### Scenario 1 — Owner-hosted private dinner

The resident owner occasionally invites up to four personally selected guests to
a meal in the home or on the existing patio. The event is not advertised to the
general public and no payment, minimum donation, ticket, lodging bundle, deposit,
or purchase is required.

Please state whether this fact pattern is treated as ordinary residential social
activity and identify any frequency, advertising, chef, food-preparation, or other
facts that would change that conclusion.

### Scenario 2 — Small prepaid smokehouse supper pilot

- Up to 10 guests at one shared table
- Up to eight specified summer evenings initially
- Fixed-price, prepaid, reservation-only admission
- No walk-ins, amplified entertainment, general event rental, or alcohol reliance
- Up to two workers
- Food prepared and served only through the pathway approved by El Paso County
  Public Health, potentially using a licensed caterer or commissary
- Listed outdoor smoker and temporary covered finishing/service setup
- Existing onsite driveway and parking, subject to required review
- Proposed portable toilet immediately north of the existing barn, subject to
  accessibility, sanitation, servicing, screening, and agency approval
- No permanent commercial-food construction during the pilot

Please identify whether this is an eating/drinking establishment, business event
center, temporary use, agritainment, or another use; whether it is allowed in
RR-5; and the application and site requirements needed before operation.

### Scenario 3 — Permanent Kiva pavilion

Only after a successful lawful pilot, the owner may propose a 400–600 square-foot
permitted food pavilion immediately west of the house, beyond the existing open
patio. The initial planning case tests approximately 36 guests on 40 evenings per
year, but no such capacity or frequency is assumed to be allowed. The structure
would have a secured exterior smoker yard and would be designed for lawful
conversion to a residential accessory studio, workshop, or garden pavilion if
the food use ends.

Please identify the land-use classification, whether it is available in RR-5,
the required discretionary approval or rezoning, and any required site
development plan, parking, access, traffic, lighting, noise, smoke/odor, water,
wastewater, drainage, wildfire, fire-district, or neighborhood review.

## Specific clarification requested

1. Does use of a licensed caterer change the land-use classification or only the
   food-license requirements?
2. Does describing a payment as a suggested or minimum donation change zoning
   treatment? What about bundling supper with lodging or another purchase?
3. Is any temporary-use path available for the eight-evening pilot, and can it be
   repeated in later years?
4. Could supper qualify as supplemental agritainment only with a bona fide
   agricultural use, and is that category available on this RR-5 parcel?
5. Would any paid supper constitute a business event center in this district?
6. What application secures a determination on which the owner may rely?
7. What current fees and additional submissions would follow from each available
   path?

Please reference the controlling code provisions and note any facts that require
a separate administrative determination. We welcome correction of any zoning or
parcel information stated above.
