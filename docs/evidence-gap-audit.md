# Kiva Evidence Gap Audit

## Audit result

Kiva has a coherent feasibility design and decision framework. It is not yet a
permit-ready building plan or a proven business. The controlling gaps are written
agency classifications, surveyed site facts, equipment and utility selections,
vendor quotes, lawful operating results, demand evidence, and an independent
property-value opinion.

The terms below are used consistently:

- **Designed:** the project has a documented working approach.
- **Researched:** the approach is supported by current published rules or market
  references.
- **Proven:** the responsible outside authority, professional, vendor, or measured
  operating record has supplied the required evidence.

## Current controlling gates

The next capital decision is **not** whether to build Kiva. It is whether the
property has any lawful and economically testable operating path.

### Gate 1 — Planning

Required artifact: issued Zoning Verification Letter answering the four-person
private dinner, eight-night paid pilot, permanent pavilion, and bona fide club
scenarios.

Spend allowed before closure: nominal research and determination costs only. No
public sale, memberships offered as event access, permanent design, or site work.

### Gate 2 — Public Health

Required artifacts: written response on the nonprofit/event issue and written
classification of the selected food model.

Spend allowed before closure: menu documentation and ordinary recipe trials. No
public food service or fixed commercial equipment.

### Gate 3 — Site and utilities

Required artifacts: survey, well evidence, OWTS/grease determination, and fire/
building pre-design responses.

Spend allowed before closure: refundable or reusable pilot equipment within the
approved Phase 1 ceiling. No excavation, utility extension, smoker enclosure, or
permit drawings.

### Gate 4 — Operating and demand evidence

Required artifacts: three rehearsals, 12 permitted services, actual cost and
labor records, price conversion, repeat/referral behavior, and mature-format
presales at the required economics.

Spend allowed before closure: approved seasonal operation. No permanent Kiva
construction.

### Gate 5 — Permanent investment

Required artifacts: approved entitlement route, schematic/code package, vendor
estimate, at least 35 percent operating contribution after ordinary annual fixed
operating costs, revenue above the $150,000 absolute floor and approximately
$190,000 at current cost/capacity assumptions, supported capital recovery, and
independent property-value/finance/insurance evidence.

## Evidence storage convention

Save each outside response with its original date and source. Record:

- authority or vendor;
- responder and title;
- property and scenario reviewed;
- question asked;
- answer and qualifications;
- expiration or code edition;
- next required submission;
- quoted cost and validity date; and
- repository document or attachment location.

Verbal guidance is useful for routing but does not close a gate. Confirm material
answers in writing.

## Audit conclusion

The project has enough definition to request agency classifications without
paying for a full design engagement. It does not have enough evidence to sell
meals, form a club for regulatory purposes, purchase specialized equipment, or
commission permit drawings. The issued zoning letter is the first controlling
piece of evidence.
