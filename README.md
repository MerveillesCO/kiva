# Kiva

Kiva is the Merveilles smokehouse concept: a reservation-led hearth kitchen and
garden supper program at 21085 Capella Drive, Monument, Colorado.

The working strategy is **prove the food and demand before building the
smokehouse**. Initial service should use a licensed commissary and an approved
temporary service setup. A permanent Kiva building proceeds only after land-use,
health, fire, building, water, wastewater, and financial gates are cleared.

## Current decisions

- Jurisdiction: unincorporated El Paso County, not Douglas County
- Fire authority: Tri-Lakes Monument Fire Protection District
- Launch capacity assumption: 6–10 prepaid guests per dinner
- Signature product: a reservation-counted smokehouse board
- Food cost planning allowance: $24 per confirmed guest
- Smoking is for flavor with continuous temperature control; no curing,
  preservation, or reduced-oxygen packaging in the launch model
- No construction or public sale before the required written approvals

All designs, costs, capacities, and uses remain preliminary until verified by the
responsible agencies and vendors.
